Our Craft

The work behind the chair.

Nancy's Salon and Spa sits on Strawberry Road in Pasadena, and it has quietly become the place locals recommend to each other. The salon covers the full beauty routine in one visit: colour and extensions, blowouts, nails, facials, lashes, brows and bridal.

The approach is simple. Listen first, look at the hair and skin in front of you, then do the work properly and take the time it takes. No rushing, no guesswork, and no look you did not ask for.

Booking today runs through Booksy or Fresha. This page is a preview of what a proper home on the web could look like, with booking by WhatsApp, real hours, and every service in one place.
